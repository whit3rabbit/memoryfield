## Answer
WMT 2014 EN-DE: 28.4 BLEU (prior SOTA 26.4).
WMT 2014 EN-FR: 41.0 BLEU (prior SOTA 40.4, ByteNet).

The +2.0 BLEU on EN-DE was the headline result; the smaller +0.6 on EN-FR is what initially convinced skeptics.
