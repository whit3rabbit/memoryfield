## Answer
BERT-base: L=12 layers, H=768 hidden, A=12 heads, 110M params.
BERT-large: L=24, H=1024, A=16, 340M params.

These configurations are reference points — many later models (RoBERTa, DistilBERT, ALBERT) are scaled variants of one or the other.
